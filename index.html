Research threads

01
First-author preprint, Zenodo, May 2026

Ro-SVD: position-aware KV-cache compression

Online low-rank compression of pre-RoPE keys in grouped-query attention, with per-head adaptive rank allocation, a custom CUDA de-rotation kernel, and a randomised SVD path. Over 1,602 samples on all 16 LongBench subsets (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, 4-bit NF4, dual T4): 73.91% ROUGE-L win rate against the uncompressed baseline.

What I learned

Python-loop SVD added 29.5% latency, so the algorithm was not the whole story: the implementation had to be batched and randomised. Head-level analysis mattered too, since a few high-entropy heads need more rank than the rest.

02
IIIT Kancheepuram, Prof. Rajesh Kolluri, 2026 to now

Safe reinforcement learning for vehicle-to-grid control

A differentiable residual-Dyna world model: an analytical AC power-flow base plus a learned residual, a quadratic-programming safety projection, and personalised federated learning. Early results are promising.

What I learned

Safety constraints belong in the architecture, not the reward. The projection layer guarantees feasible actions, so the learned residual only has to correct the physics model.

04
Multi-agent harness, 2026

Sentinel: cross-modal red-teaming

Probes LLMs across five attack categories and measures how jailbreak success shifts between text, image, and video delivery, compared with McNemar and Wilcoxon tests. Code is private for now.

What I learned

Raw pass rates mislead. Paired tests changed which differences I trusted, and the modality gap only became visible once delivery format was held as the single changing variable.
